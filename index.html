📈 About ChromaVision

ChromaVision helps you create charts that are readable by everyone — including people with color vision deficiency, which affects approximately 8% of males and 0.5% of females.

Features
  • ✅ 8 chart types (bar, line, pie, scatter…)
  • ✅ 7 colorblind-safe palettes (incl. Cividis)
  • ✅ Pattern fills for shape-based distinction
  • ✅ Vision simulation for 5 deficiency types
  • ✅ WCAG 2.1 contrast scoring badge
  • ✅ Animated insight ring + mini bar chart
  • ✅ 5 quick-load sample datasets
  • ✅ Random data generator
  • ✅ CSV import & drag-drop
  • ✅ PNG, JPG, CSV & Print export
  • ✅ High Contrast mode
  • ✅ Keyboard shortcuts (G, T, R, 1–4)
  • ✅ Before/After comparison labels
  • ✅ Light & dark & high-contrast themes
  • ✅ Fully responsive design
Color Blindness Types
Protanopia — Red-blind, ~1% of males
Deuteranopia — Green-blind, ~1% of males
Tritanopia — Blue-blind, rare
Achromatopsia — No color perception, very rare
